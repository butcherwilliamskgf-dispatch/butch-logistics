# Butch Logistics | US Truck Dispatch & Carrier Intelligence Suite

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new)

An all-in-one platform built for independent US truck dispatchers, owner-operators, and carrier vetting operations. Combines the best elements of **Roadway Logistics** (client acquisition & driver onboarding), **CarrierIntel** (real-time FMCSA MC/DOT intelligence & Carrier Cabinet CRM), and **FreightLens** (automated W-9, Authority Certificate, and COI document generation).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Client Dispatch Portfolio (Roadway Logistics Model)**
   - High-impact dark mode aesthetic with cinematic American trucking backdrops.
   - Live performance badges ($7,850+ avg weekly gross, $3.12/mi, 100% no-forced dispatch, 24/7 support).
   - Dedicated service tiers for 53' Dry Van (6%), 53' Reefer (7%), and Flatbed/Step Deck (8%).
   - **Quick 60-Second Driver Sign-Up**: Big-touch visual equipment buttons, one-click MC auto-fill from FMCSA, and direct phone shortcut `(209) 202-3080`.

2. **Automated MC Packet Generator (FreightLens Model)**
   - Enter any MC# or DOT# to auto-generate:
     - **Pre-filled IRS Form W-9** (Box 1 Legal Name, Box 2 DBA, Federal Tax Class, Address, matched EIN).
     - **FMCSA Authority Certificate** (Docket grant date & active authority status).
     - **Certificate of Insurance (COI) Verification Dossier** ($1M liability verification + 1-click ACORD 25 agent request).
   - One-click ZIP packet bundle export.

3. **Live MC Data Extractor & Carrier Cabinet (CarrierIntel Model)**
   - Instant query engine for any MC# or USDOT#.
   - Real-time verification of operating authority, BIPD liability limits ($1,000,000), safety rating, and inspection OOS percentages.
   - Interactive Carrier Cabinet CRM table to monitor and export vetted carriers.

4. **Document OCR Parser**
   - Neural OCR parser for carrier onboarding paperwork (W-9 forms & MC Authority letters) with automatic FMCSA SAFER cross-verification.
